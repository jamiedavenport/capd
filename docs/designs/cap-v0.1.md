## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x version is "everything you've ever seen, one hotkey away" — always-on auto-capture of browsing history with full archiving and semantic recall (the Rewind/mymind direction). Named and deliberately NOT pursued: it trades the sharp Pinboard wedge for a privacy-heavy always-on product. It stays vision-only.

### Platform Potential
CapKit's `ProcessingStep` pipeline is the long-term moat: opening it to third-party steps in v0.2+ turns cap from an app into the OSS capture substrate other tools build on.

## Accepted Scope (added to this plan)
- E1: Fetch the full page over the network on link capture and extract readable body text; store and FTS-index it. **What E1 adds beyond the design doc:** the design doc's v0.1 pipeline "text extraction" covered only content already in hand (selected text, og-metadata from the title fetch); E1 fetches and indexes the whole page body. Implementation route: Readability.js in an offscreen WKWebView (handles JS-heavy pages), SwiftSoup heuristics as the lightweight fallback — hence M-L, not M. Fail-soft: on extraction failure the capture keeps title/URL/selection and marks `body_status=failed` (retryable via `cap refetch`). **Fetch policy (decided in this review, question D4): on by default, disclosed in README/FAQ, settings toggle + `cap add --no-fetch` opt-out.**
- E2: Vision-framework OCR pipeline step for image captures; OCR text FTS-indexed. (Pulled forward from the design doc's v0.2 slot — see Design Doc Amendments.)
- E4: README one-line positioning ("why this instead of Linkding/Anybox?"), 10-second capture→recall demo GIF, Show HN post draft — all before the v0.1 tag.
- E5: Raycast extension (TypeScript, calls cap CLI): search captures, open result. Lives in the cap repo under `raycast/`. **Sequencing:** built after the dogfood-week gate passes; Raycast store submission waits on the CLI-name resolution (design doc Open Question 3) and store review adds calendar latency independent of build effort.

Accepted scope adds roughly one week of evenings (CC: a few hours). Sequencing against the design doc's gates:
- **Pre-dogfood (with the CLI core):** E1 and E2 — they are CapKit pipeline steps and must be in the dogfood build, since the dogfood week is supposed to test recall quality (premise 1) and recall quality is exactly what E1/E2 change.
- **Post-dogfood (launch phase):** E4 and E5.
- The "daily-drivable within 3 weeks" criterion moves to **~4 weeks** (evenings scale); unchanged at CC scale. The dogfood gate itself is unchanged.
