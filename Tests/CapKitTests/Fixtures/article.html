Migratory Patterns of the Alpine Chough

Field notes on the migratory patterns of the alpine chough, collected across four seasons of ridge surveys in the eastern ranges.

The survey teams walked the ridge line every morning before the wind picked up, logging each sighting in a shared notebook that lived in the hut. Over four seasons the notebook filled with small corrections, and the corrections turned out to matter more than the counts themselves when the time came to compare years.
